Goal

Score the most points by collecting coloured tiles and decorating your wall. The game ends when at least one player completes a full horizontal row on their wall.

Setup

Five factory displays are each filled with 4 random tiles drawn from a bag of 100 tiles (20 of each colour). The centre pool starts with the first-player marker.

On Your Turn

  1. Pick all tiles of one colour from any single factory display or from the centre pool.
  2. If you take from a factory, the leftover tiles of other colours move to the centre.
  3. If you take from the centre and the first-player marker is there, it moves to your floor line (−1 penalty, but you go first next round).
  4. Place the tiles on one of your five pattern lines. Each row only holds one colour and fills right to left. Overflow tiles go to your floor line.

Pattern Line Rules

  • Row 1 holds 1 tile, row 2 holds 2, row 3 holds 3, and so on.
  • A row can only hold one colour at a time.
  • You cannot place a colour in a row if that colour is already on your wall in that row.
  • You can send tiles straight to your floor instead of a pattern line.

End of Round

When all factories and the centre are empty, the round ends automatically.

  • Each completed pattern line moves one tile onto your wall and scores points. The rest go to the discard lid.
  • Incomplete pattern lines stay for next round.
  • Floor penalties are applied: −1, −1, −2, −2, −2, −3, −3. Your score never drops below 0.

Scoring a Tile

  • A tile placed alone scores 1 point.
  • If it connects to other tiles horizontally, score the full horizontal run.
  • If it connects vertically, score the full vertical run.
  • If it connects both ways, score both runs added together.

End Game Bonuses

When the game ends, bonus points are added:

  • +2 for each complete horizontal row on your wall.
  • +7 for each complete vertical column on your wall.
  • +10 for each colour where all five tiles appear on your wall.

Winning

The player with the most points wins. If scores are tied, the player with more complete rows wins. If still tied, it is a draw.
