Azul

Namespace

Azul

Description:

  • Azul.js is a module to model and play the tile-drafting board game Azul Players take turns drafting coloured tiles from factory displays into their pattern lines. Completed rows score points and decorate their wall. The game ends when any player completes a full horizontal row on the wall. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Azul_(board_game)
